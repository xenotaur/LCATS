# Opening editorial evidence: a partial finding is not story approval

Status: evidence checkpoint, not an acceptance-policy change. WI-GETTENBERG-0010
and PR #524 remain open. The human authorized this bounded investigation and narrow
implementation; the future choices below are recommendations requiring a new grant.

## What the cached sources establish

All 1,659 population inputs remain accounted for, including 1,640 candidates. A
bounded locator for complete, explicitly delimited publisher/editor notes finds two
candidates. Independent source inspection supports promotional roles for both:
63638 (Electron Eat Electron) and 73988 (Electro-episoded in A.D. 2025). Exact source
spans, original labels and attributed review records now have machine-verifiable
source/candidate/metadata/assessment bindings. Software does not verify the semantic
truth of the role judgment. The complete notes can be preserved separately
using the existing v2 publisher_blurb metadata representation.

Both stories remain unresolved. 63638 retains other front/end matter; 73988 retains
an illustration caption and other historical differences. No complete historical
explanation or story approval follows from these two partial findings. Counts remain
14 resolved /1,628 unresolved; all 55 dispositions, including 35 deferrals, are intact.

The available 27 HTML cache resources do not cover any of the 25 earlier diagnostic
cases. Those openings remain unclassified. A broader exploratory search of opening
source context also found a mixed publishing-history preface in 42664, which is not
forced into the promotional family, and counterexamples such as attributed epigraphs,
fictional introductions and narrative references to editors. Formatting and position
cannot distinguish these roles reliably.

The locator scans at most 8,192 candidate characters and requires complete finite
note forms. Its two matches are not an estimate of how many genuine blurbs exist.
There were no primary-magazine scans acquired and no new network acquisition. Cached
source claims are not authenticated original publisher authorship. The investigation
does not claim a complete literary reading of the corpus.

## Why another identical run will not scale

The full population replay takes about 31 seconds. The limiting resource is positive
role evidence and the collection's treatment of that material, not evaluator speed
or agent concurrency. This run supplies a reusable partial-evidence mechanism but
qualifies no broad source family for automatic editorial classification.

delivery rather than full corpus curation, separately decide whether WI0010 should
allow that separation; do not infer it from green software tests.
