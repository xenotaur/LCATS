# Work Item: WI-SF-0007

## Summary

Preregister the approximately 30-story Phase 2 feasibility pilot, including deterministic sampling, annotation procedure, audit design, and pass/fail thresholds.

## Problem / Context

The proposal requires a gated Phase 2 pilot before scaling to the Worldcon sample, with thresholds declared before observing pilot results (`project/design/proposals/adopted/knight-novum-analysis-sidecar/00_proposal.md:506`). This planning item exists to keep sample design and gates separate from runtime implementation and pilot execution.

## Scope

- Define the deterministic approximately 30-story sample.
- Predeclare annotation, adjudication, audit, repeated-run stability, and whole-story/chunked comparison procedures.
- Predeclare quantitative and qualitative thresholds before execution.

## Required Changes

1. Create `experimental/science_fiction_analysis_trial/phase2_preregistration.md`.
2. Create `experimental/science_fiction_analysis_trial/manifests/phase2_sample_manifest.json`.
3. Document clear SF positives, boundary/adjacent cases, and negative controls.
4. Document human annotation and adjudication rules, audit subsets, repeated-run stability checks, whole-story versus chunked comparisons, metrics, and thresholds.

## Non-Goals

- Do not run the Phase 2 pilot.
- Do not make paid model calls.
- Do not inspect pilot results before thresholds are frozen.
- Do not write corpus sidecars.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Phase 2 sample manifest is deterministic and contains approximately 10 clear SF positives, 10 boundary or adjacent cases, and 10 negative controls.
- Human annotation, adjudication, stratified audit, repeated-run stability, whole-story versus chunked comparison, and reporting procedures are preregistered.
- Thresholds for evidence validity, agreement, recall, failures, latency, token cost, dollar cost, and review time are declared before pilot execution.
- The artifact contains no paid calls, no pilot results, and no corpus writes.
