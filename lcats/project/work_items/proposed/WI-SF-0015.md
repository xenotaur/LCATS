# Work Item: WI-SF-0015

## Summary

Run a bounded repeated two-story canary that verifies the hardened Knight and Suvin contracts across a positive and a negative/adjacent control before reconsidering the 10-story or 146-story runs.

## Problem / Context

The previous 10-story runs demonstrated useful feasibility but also exposed provider-specific output shapes, truncation, and partial-stage failures. A two-story contrastive canary is a smaller and more interpretable test of whether the hardened input/output path behaves as intended. It is an operational contract check, not a theoretical accuracy study or human-annotation substitute.

The existing manifest has positive, boundary, and negative smoke roles, while the captured Opus output provides a clear positive candidate (`A Case of Sunburn`) and an adjacent negative control (`Anderson - The Bell`). This item creates a dedicated pair and repeats it enough to expose instability without escalating to the 10- or 146-story sample.

### Duplication search

- In-repo: Related: `WI-SF-0012` and its existing smoke/sample results; no dedicated repeated contract-canary item found.
- Sibling repos: None identified.
- External libraries: None identified; pytest and the existing experiment runner are sufficient.
- Recommendation: Proceed with a dedicated experiment-local canary rather than reusing the broader 10-story sample as a contract test.

### Demand search

- Work items: `WI-SF-0012` requires a smoke-before-sample gate but does not define this post-hardening canary; `WI-SF-0008` and `WI-SF-0010` are later pilot executions.
- Proposals: The adopted Knight/Novum proposal requires experiment-local validation before scale but does not duplicate this specific canary.
- Backlog: No separate contract-canary demand found.
- Recommendation: Link this item to the existing spike and keep later pilots unchanged.

## Scope

- Add a deterministic two-story canary manifest and experiment-local run layout.
- Run three no-cost/local trials, inspecting each trial before any paid run.
- Optionally run up to two paid Opus trials under a separately reviewed small budget.
- Compare structural, normalization, persistence, failure, latency, and semantic-expectation outcomes.

## Required Changes

1. Create `experimental/science_fiction_analysis_trial/manifests/contract_canary_manifest.json` selecting `mass_quantities/a_case_of_sunburn__fontenay` and `anderson/bell`.
2. Create trial-specific output roots under `results/worldcon_spike/contract_canary/<backend>/<trial>/`.
3. Run no-cost local-model trials first, with `max_failures=3`, preserving raw outputs, logs, checkpoints, sidecars, and validation results. Fixture mode may cover structural contract tests, but it must not count as a semantic canary unless its outputs are explicitly contrastive and case-specific.
4. Inspect every local trial for exact seven-criterion Knight structure, valid evidence references, N/C/H behavior, repair/coercion findings, and byte-safe persistence before proceeding.
5. Prepare a paid approval package only if local trials are structurally healthy; name backend/model, configuration, estimated tokens/cost/time, output root, budget, and stop conditions.
6. Run no more than two paid Opus trials and no more than five total two-story trials.
7. Produce `contract_canary_report.md` with per-trial comparison, failures, repairs, costs, latency, and a proceed/revise/stop recommendation.

## Non-Goals

- Do not run the 10-story or 146-story sample.
- Do not perform human annotation, adjudication, or gold-label creation.
- Do not claim theoretical accuracy, inter-annotator agreement, or Phase 2 validation.
- Do not change rubric definitions based only on these two stories.
- Do not write corpus sidecars or modify production promotion/annotation commands.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The canary manifest contains exactly one expected positive and one expected negative/adjacent control with deterministic story hashes.
- Three local/no-cost trials are attempted first and every trial has persisted raw, normalized, log, checkpoint, and validation artifacts.
- Semantic canary trials use a real local model, or a documented contrastive fixture whose outputs differ by case; an all-positive fixture cannot be used to evaluate the negative control.
- Any paid Opus trial is preceded by an explicit reviewed approval package and remains within the approved small budget.
- The positive case has at least one present Knight criterion and one qualified N/C/H candidate; the negative case has no qualified novum, without requiring an exact Knight interval.
- No malformed output is silently treated as a successful complete analysis; all coercions, repairs, invalid references, and quarantines are reported.
- The canary report gives a clear proceed/revise/stop recommendation and `lrh validate` passes with 0 errors.

## Dependencies / Order

This item follows `WI-SF-0013` and `WI-SF-0014`. It is the final gate in this hardening tranche and must complete before any decision to rerun the 10-story sample or prepare a 146-story approval package.

## Risk Notes

- Two stories cannot establish theoretical accuracy or generalization; the report must describe the canary as a contract and feasibility check only.
- Repeated identical trials measure stability more than independent variation, especially at temperature zero.
- Prior outputs are useful for selecting cases but must not become unexamined gold labels.
- Paid trials may exceed the simple linear estimate after higher token limits or retries; approval must use a conservative budget.
