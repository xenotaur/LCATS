# Work Item: WI-SF-0131

## Summary

Run the date-aware stage live on a local model for the seven canary stories and two new controls, compare it with the hand trial and with the existing Heinlein record, and report what the stage does and where it should not be trusted.

## Problem / Context

The proposal's design is supported by six hand-classified stories by one rater, which is not a measurement. Open Question 7 sets the bar for a first live run, which the owner kept: the model's premise lists match the hand trial closely enough that the owner accepts the disagreements; the lists are stable across three trials; and model-asserted dates are reviewed against the fact table where it covers them. The owner's decision on setting time adds two controls: a historical-fiction story faithful to the author's own history (which should give `different: absent`) and an alternate-history story with a stipulated divergence (which should give a `counterfactual_divergence` premise and `different: present`). Open Question 5 settled that `human` is copied from the existing record in the first implementation and compared here. The infrastructure is in place: per-attempt request records (WI-SF-0120), the private-server runbook with isolation and sleep checks (WI-SF-0121), and the WI-SF-0119 and WI-SF-0116 lessons on identical inputs with differing answers.

### Duplication search
- In-repo: WI-SF-0119 and WI-SF-0116 ran the five-criterion canary; this is the date-aware stage, a different record. Recommendation: proceed.

### Demand search
- The proposal's Implementation Plan item 6. Recommendation: no action.

## Scope

- Live runs on a local loopback model only, a committed report, and the evidence files.

## Required Changes

1. Choose the two controls with the owner and record their composition dates with cited sources or owner overrides.
2. Run the stage three times per story on a private server per the runbook, saving request records and checking isolation and sleep.
3. Compare premise lists with the hand trial; check stability across trials; review model-asserted dates against the fact table; compare `human`, `essential` and `causal` with the existing record.
4. Measure the extra stage's calls and cost, write the report with the recommendation, and commit the results as evidence.

## Non-Goals

- No paid runs, no change to the stage, rubric or prompt of existing stages, and no decision on the five-criterion prompt (WI-SF-0132).
- No claim that the design is validated beyond the evidence collected.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The run covers the seven canary stories and at least one historical-fiction control and one alternate-history control, three trials each, on a private loopback server following the runbook, with per-attempt request records, the isolation and sleep checks, and every date either cited to an opened source or supplied by the owner
- The report compares the model premise lists with the six-story hand trial and states, per story, the agreements and the disagreements for the owner to accept or reject, and states whether the lists are stable across the three trials
- Model-asserted dates are reviewed against the fact table where it covers them, and each disagreement is listed
- The report compares the new stage `human`, `essential` and `causal` statuses with the existing Heinlein record and lists every difference with the reason the author-time frame explains it, or states that it does not
- The report records the call count and measured cost of the extra stage, states that the evidence is a small set and not a corpus sample, and gives a recommendation for the owner without claiming validation beyond what the runs show
- No paid call is made, `lrh validate` reports 0 errors, and persisted results are committed as evidence

## Validation

- `lrh validate`
- `git diff --check`
- `PYTHONPATH=src scripts/test`

## Dependencies / Order

Depends on `WI-SF-0127` (the stage), `WI-SF-0128` (dates) and `WI-SF-0129` (the audit table); `WI-SF-0120` and `WI-SF-0121` are resolved. Blocks `WI-SF-0132`.

## Risk Notes

- A local model gave identical inputs and differing answers at temperature 0.0 in WI-SF-0116; three trials per story is the minimum and a single run is not decision-grade.
- The machine can sleep during a long run; follow the runbook's sleep check and do not misdiagnose a sleep as a hang.
- Another session may be using the shared Ollama server; use the private server and never stop the other session's server.
- Choosing controls after seeing a result would bias the report; fix the controls and their expected outcomes before running.
