# Work Item: WI-SF-0128

## Summary

Build the loading and intersection of composition-date sources for the date-aware Heinlein stage, and evaluate a bibliographic source's coverage and terms before anything relies on it.

## Problem / Context

The corpus records a story's year as Unknown (`src/lcats/gatherers/parser.py:1457`), so dates must be sourced. The proposal sets the order of sources and treats the story's own first publication as an upper bound on composition, not a lower bound; the author's death date is a hard upper bound and career milestones are context only. In the six-story trial several dates rested on sources that were not opened, and A Case of Sunburn's April 1957 date came from audiobook listings only. Owner decision Q8: the date source is evaluated by this item, not decided in the proposal, and the owner's override always wins.

### Duplication search
- In-repo: no date tooling exists. Recommendation: proceed.

### Demand search
- The proposal's Implementation Plan item 3. Recommendation: no action.

## Scope

- Owner-supplied date loading and override, interval construction and intersection (pure code with tests), and a written evaluation of a bibliographic source.

## Required Changes

1. Define a small reviewable file format for owner-supplied composition dates and load it; the override replaces the computed result and is recorded.
2. Implement interval intersection over the proposal's sources with per-source basis and confidence, tested with the six trial stories' known cases (including a source that gives only an upper bound).
3. Evaluate ISFDB (or another named bibliographic source): coverage on the six trial stories and the Worldcon pilot stories, terms and rate limits, and the verdict; cite each checked date to its source and label unconfirmed ones.
4. Record the evaluation as a dated report under `experimental/science_fiction_analysis_trial/`.

## Non-Goals

- No bulk scraping and no automatic network lookup inside the pipeline; any lookup used in the evaluation is manual and bounded.
- No change to the stage or contract beyond what WI-SF-0126 defines.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Owner-supplied composition dates (earliest, latest, basis, caveats) can be loaded from a reviewable file and override every other source, and the override is recorded in the composition record
- The composition interval is built from the sources in the proposal order (owner-supplied, bibliographic first-publication as an upper bound, author lifespan with birth as lower and death as hard upper bound, internal evidence), intersected, with each source recorded with kind, value, basis and confidence, and an unknown date gives an unbounded interval and never a guess
- A written evaluation of at least one bibliographic source (ISFDB is the candidate) states its coverage on the six trial stories and the Worldcon pilot set, its terms of use, and a verdict on whether it may be relied on, with each date checked against a cited source
- No external service is queried by tests, and `lrh validate` reports 0 errors

## Validation

## Dependencies / Order

Depends on `WI-SF-0126`. Blocks WI-SF-0131 (the live run needs dates for its stories).

## Risk Notes

- Terms of use may forbid programmatic use of a source; the verdict must say so rather than work around it.
- A date that is wrong by a few years can flip a time-sensitive story (A Case of Sunburn flips at Mariner 4, 15 July 1965), so every date used in the live run needs a cited source or an owner override.
